# FAQ

The plugin's Frequently Asked Questions — shortcodes, exchange rate updates, per-product fixed prices, WooCommerce HPOS compatibility, the conversion endpoint's rate limit, and more — are answered in the FAQ section of [readme.txt on GitHub](https://github.com/MaxHandMade/mhm-currency-switcher/blob/v2.0.0/readme.txt).

The Turkish version of this page carries the full list, merged from readme.txt and this guide, including a few questions this guide's readers ask that readme.txt does not cover — such as what happens to your data if you delete the plugin.
